How Connectly works

One marketplace for brands and creators in India — from discovery to paid delivery, with a clear deal timeline.

For creators
  • Free profile in the directory
  • Apply to open campaigns in one click
  • Receive brand deal offers by email
  • Upload work & get paid via Connectly
For brands
  • Search creators by niche, city & followers
  • Send deal offers with price & brief
  • Post campaigns for creators to apply
  • Pay once to Connectly — we release to creator

End-to-end flow

1

Sign up once

Google sign-in → choose Creator or Brand → complete your profile. One account, one role.

2

Discover & match

Brands browse the creator directory or post a campaign. Creators apply to open campaigns or wait for invites.

3

Deal offer

Brand sends price, deliverable, deadline & brief. Creator sees it in My Deals and can Accept or Decline.

4

Create & pay

Creator uploads work. Brand pays Connectly (UPI ref). Connectly confirms payment, then reviews / brand approves.

5

Payout

Connectly pays the creator. Creator confirms receipt. Deal closes — everything stays on the timeline.
